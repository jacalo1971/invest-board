What is the stock market?

Three ideas to get started.

A share is a tiny piece of a company

If you buy one share of NVIDIA, you own a tiny slice of NVIDIA. When the company does well, your slice is worth more.

The price goes up and down every day

When lots of people want to buy, the price goes up; when lots want to sell, it goes down. That is why it changes every minute the market is open.

Investing is a patience game

On any given day the market can drop. Over many years, it has historically gone up. That is why you only invest money you won't need soon.

Golden rules

What good investors do.

Don't put all your eggs in one basketSpread your money across several companies or an ETF. If one falls, the others protect you.
Invest in what you understandIf you can't explain what the company does in one sentence, research it before buying.
Think in years, not daysOne-day drops are normal. What matters is where the company is heading long term.
Only money you won't needNever invest money meant for rent, school or an emergency.
Little by littleInvesting a fixed amount every month means you don't have to worry about the "perfect moment".
